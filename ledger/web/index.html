        RESEARCH WORKFLOW
        
        
        How it works
        
        
        Follow a research question from its sources to a thesis you can revisit.
        
        
        
          
        From evidence to thesis
        
          
          
            
        1. 01Gather evidence
          Save dated prices, financials, headlines, and relevant prior theses.
          2. 
            
        2. 02Analyze in parallel
          Specialists review technicals, fundamentals, news, and sentiment.
          3. 
            
        3. 03Debate
          Bull and bear agents make opposing cases and cite their evidence.
          4. 
            
        4. 04Synthesize and review risk
          The manager weighs both cases and the price scenarios. The risk reviewer can abstain.
          5. 
            
        5. 05Save the thesis
          Keep the report and a short memory with its sources and invalidation criteria.
          6. 
          
        
          
        
            
        Forecast → step 04. Price scenarios enter at synthesis, after the specialists and debate have formed their views.
        
            
        Thesis Ledger → step 01. A later run can retrieve relevant prior theses. Review can narrow or retire a memory.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            
        What each view does
        
            
        Research desk shows the market, company headlines, specialist reports, debate, and risk review. The Evidence Room opens the sources behind a report.
        
            
        Research chat answers questions using market tools and saved reports, and can run the analyst team when asked.
        
            
        Forecast samples price scenarios from completed daily bars. Forecast Checks compares the historical-return model with a no-change baseline.
        
            
        Thesis Ledger keeps short, revisable research memories. Research History stores full reports and lets you resume an interrupted run.
        
          
        
          
        
            
        Where the data comes from
        
            
        Prices. Alpaca supplies quotes and intraday candles. Alpha Vantage supplies daily bars, with Alpaca as a fallback.
        
            
        Financials. Alpha Vantage supplies company metrics. SEC EDGAR supplies figures from filed quarterly and annual reports when the overview is unavailable.
        
            
        Headlines. Company coverage comes from Alpaca and Alpha Vantage. Market context comes from CNBC Markets, the Federal Reserve, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
        
            
        Sentiment. The analyst compares FINRA short-sale activity with recent sessions and uses ticker-specific news tone when available. When configured, Grok X Search adds cited posts from company and industry accounts. The report treats these as selected commentary.
